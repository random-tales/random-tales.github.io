Hello there! :wave: I'm Vale, and this is my blog. Here is where I collect notes on the AI/ML ideas I find fascinating. Most grow out of my research; some are just curiosity. :hugs:

I'm a researcher at [Ericsson](https://www.ericsson.com/en), exploring how AI/ML can power the next generation of video compression technologies. :office:

Before that, I've had the privilege of working as a research assistant at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de/en/) <span>
  <img src="/assets/tum.png" alt="tum" style="width: 1em; cursor: default; overflow: hidden; position: relative; top: 3px;">
</span>, specifically at the [Chair of Signal Processing (MSV)](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/msv/home/). There, I developed deep learning models based on transformers and graph neural networks for the physical layer of wireless communications. I also studied cross-domain generalization in neural networks, using kernel-based and statistical methods to explain robust transfer across distribution shifts without target-domain data. This research culminated in my Dr.-Ing. *summa cum laude* from TUM in 2025 (see [dissertation](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1764282/1764282.pdf)). :mortar_board:

During my doctorate, I collaborated with [Futurewei Technologies](https://www.futurewei.com/) (USA) and [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/) to develop AI-driven solutions for next-generation wireless systems. At the same time, I supported the Numerical Linear Algebra for Signal Processing course in the Master’s program by conducting tutorials (exercise lectures) and preparing programming projects. :woman_teacher:

I hold a BSc and an MSc (both with honors) in Telecommunication Engineering from [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it/en/). During my master’s studies, I did an exchange and wrote my master's thesis at TUM, while also working as a signal processing student at [Intel Corporation](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/homepage.html) during the "5G era". :nerd_face:

I’m a big fan of Weights & Biases (wandb), an awesome tool for tracking ML experiments. Feel free to check my [ML portfolio](https://wandb.ai/angus27) there!
